## [Initial Public Release] — 2026

### Added

- Tracking for all 195 countries
- Interactive map and continent statistics
- Travel timeline, dates and achievements
- Home-screen map widget
- Premium travel notes and custom map themes
- Supporter badges and eligible launcher icons
- Offline-first storage with no GPS tracking
- Zero advertising and analytics SDKs

### Documentation

- Added README, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Support and Security documentation.
- Added standardized GitHub issue and pull-request templates.

For the current production release, visit [PATHY on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pathydc).
