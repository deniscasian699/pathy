## Common Questions

### Travel history disappeared

PATHY has no cloud backup. Check whether app data was cleared, the app was reinstalled or the device was reset.

### A map country is hard to select

Zoom the map and try again. Include the country and device model in a bug report.

### A theme or icon did not apply

Restart the app or launcher. Some launchers cache icons and widgets.

### Premium did not restore

Use Restore Purchases with the original Google account and an active internet connection.

## Billing and Refunds

Billing is handled by Google Play. Manage subscriptions at [Google Play Subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). Refund requests are governed by [Google Play’s refund policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

Never post payment details, full order receipts, private QR content, reminder text, travel notes or other sensitive information in a public issue.
