## 🔧 Common Issues & Solutions

### 🗺️ Map Labels or Zoom
Pinch to zoom and drag to move around. The initial overview shows continents;
country labels appear as space becomes available. For an issue, include your
device, Android version, PATHY version and a screenshot.

### 📍 Multiple Visits and Cities
Use the visit dialog to enter a date and a city or area.
Suggestions are limited to the cities included in the local dataset.
If a smaller place is missing, enter its name manually.
Adding another visit preserves existing visits; removing all visits clears the country's visits.

### 📱 Widget Not Updating
Open PATHY and check your visited countries, then remove and re-add the widget if necessary.
Preview appearance and sizing can vary by launcher.
For a report, include the device model, launcher and widget size.

### 📝 Missing Travel Data
PATHY has no cloud backup. Clearing app data, uninstalling the app, or resetting
the device can permanently remove your travel records and notes.
The developer does not have a copy to restore.

### 💎 Purchase Not Activating
1. Use the Google Play account that made the purchase.
2. Open **PATHY Settings → Restore Purchases** with an internet connection.
3. If access remains missing, email support with your Google Play order ID.
   Do not put payment details or order IDs in a public GitHub issue.

### 🔔 How to Remove Ads
Purchase **Supporter / Remove Ads** for ad removal while its one-time entitlement
remains valid, or **Monthly Supporter** for ad removal during the active subscription.
**Premium Lifetime does not remove ads.** It unlocks notes, themes, icons and
the advertised premium features separately.

Refunds, entitlement revocations or subscription expiry may restore advertising
after purchase status is synchronized. Cancelling renewal normally leaves
subscription benefits available until the paid period ends.

### 🛡️ Advertising Privacy Choices
Use **Settings → Ad privacy choices** to revisit the available Google UMP choices
while advertising is enabled. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily
remove all ads. The advertising privacy entry is hidden while ad removal is active.
